Plain language

What “non-medical” means.

Seasons Care Services is non-medical companion care. That means I help with daily life: getting places, eating well, keeping the house manageable, and having company. I don't administer medication, provide nursing care, or help with bathing, dressing or toileting.

That boundary is deliberate. It keeps you safe, it keeps me inside my training, and it means I can be honest with you about when you need a licensed home health agency instead. If that day comes, I'll tell you plainly and help you find one.
